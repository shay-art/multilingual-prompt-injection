## Research Ethics

This research documents safety behavior differences across languages 
in publicly available LLM APIs. All testing is done on free 
consumer-facing tiers within each platform's terms of service.

No exploits are published against production systems. If testing 
reveals a genuine vulnerability in a third-party product, I will 
follow responsible disclosure before making anything public.

Prompts are designed to test model responses to injection-style 
inputs, not to extract genuinely harmful content.
